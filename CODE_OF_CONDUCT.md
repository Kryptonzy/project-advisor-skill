# Code of Conduct

We want Project Advisor to be a welcoming place for thoughtful, constructive engineering discussion.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, patient, and inclusive of people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Critique the ideas and evidence, not a contributor's intelligence or identity.
- Respect privacy; don't share private messages, company materials, or personally identifying information without authorization.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and repeated disruptive behavior.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, but accept feedback and correct mistakes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate comments, close threads, restrict participation, or report violations to GitHub. Decisions will be based on severity, context, and the safety of affected people.

For ordinary moderation concerns, use a repository issue without sharing private information. For a sensitive interpersonal issue, use any private contact channel explicitly published by the maintainers on their GitHub profile; if one is not available, request a private reporting route without including the sensitive details publicly.

This Code of Conduct applies to project issues, pull requests, and other official community spaces.
